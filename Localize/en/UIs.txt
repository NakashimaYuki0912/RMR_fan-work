  <text id="ui_RMR_Help_Nav_Atlas">Compendium</text>
  <text id="ui_RMR_Help_Body_Overview">[Core structure]&#10;RMR uses a funnel-shaped route. Early stages are short and relatively forgiving while resources are scarce; later stages expand the route, strengthen enemies, and demand a more complete build while offering broader and better rewards.&#10;&#10;[Route choices]&#10;Choose among normal battles, abnormality battles, bosses, shops, rests, and mysteries according to your team, pages, and resources.&#10;&#10;[Nodes and floors]&#10;Chapters 1 and 2 replace abnormality-battle nodes with rests. Switching floors in preparation only changes the map theme and music; it does not change the team or consume a reception.&#10;&#10;[Journey's end]&#10;Defeating each chapter boss advances the run. Defeating the Impuritas Civitatis boss completes the journey.</text>
  <text id="ui_RMR_Help_Body_Route">Choose the next stop after every encounter. Routes offer different combat pressure, rewards, and risks.&#10;&#10;Normal battles provide steady pages, passives, and money. Elite battles are harder but usually pay better. Boss victories advance the chapter and unlock stronger enemies, rewards, and goods. Abnormality battles award their pages; chapters one and two use rest nodes instead. Shops, rests, and mysteries provide purchases, recovery, or special choices. The Impuritas Civitatis boss ends the run.</text>
  <text id="ui_RMR_Help_Body_Rewards">[Reward types]&#10;Stages can award combat pages, key pages, abnormality pages, passives, and E.G.O. combat pages.&#10;&#10;[Normal stages]&#10;Normal stages award combat, key, and abnormality pages. The abnormality pool grows cumulatively: the early stages use Malkuth, Netzach, Yesod, and Hod; Urban Plague/Nightmare add Gebura, Tiphereth, and Chesed; Star of the City/Impuritas add Hokma, Keter, and Binah.&#10;&#10;[Combat page types]&#10;Combat pages are obtained and upgraded by type rather than as individual copies.&#10;&#10;[Emotion picks and E.G.O.]&#10;Mid-battle abnormality choices use only pages owned in the current route: team Emotion 1–2 selects tier I, 3–4 tier II, and 5 tier III. A page-name suffix is not its tier. Mid-battle E.G.O. choices are also limited to E.G.O. owned in the route.&#10;&#10;[Abnormality battles]&#10;These replace combat/key page rewards with three consecutive abnormality-page choices.&#10;&#10;[Boss battles]&#10;Bosses also offer a passive. Cleared realizations add their exclusive abnormality and E.G.O. pages to eligible boss rewards.</text>
  <text id="ui_RMR_Help_Body_Shop">[Stock]&#10;Shops sell combat pages, key pages, passives, abnormality pages, and E.G.O. combat pages, and offer combat-page upgrades. Stock depends on the chapter, realization progress, and current inventory.&#10;&#10;[Combat page upgrades]&#10;Because combat pages are owned by type, an upgrade strengthens that page type rather than one individual copy.&#10;&#10;[Upgrade price]&#10;The first upgrade costs 10 Ahn. Each successful upgrade raises the next price by 2: 10 → 12 → 14 → 16… These values may still be tuned.</text>
  <text id="ui_RMR_Help_Body_Atlas">[Recorded content]&#10;Key pages, combat pages, abnormality pages, and E.G.O. combat pages obtained during play are recorded permanently. Upgraded combat-page effects are recorded as well.&#10;&#10;[Viewing]&#10;Use the Compendium to review collected effects and current upgrade states.&#10;&#10;[Gameplay use]&#10;Compendium unlocks determine the pages available in realizations and some special stages. The permanent Compendium remains separate from current-run inventory.&#10;&#10;[Run saves]&#10;Team setup, inventory, money, chapter progress, and remaining nodes belong only to the current journey. Compendium, realization, and special-boss first-clear records persist. Continue appears only when a valid run save exists.</text>
  <text id="ui_RMR_Help_Body_Realization">[Compendium limit]&#10;All key, combat, abnormality, and E.G.O. pages used in a realization must already be recorded in the permanent Compendium.&#10;&#10;[Chapter limit]&#10;Malkuth, Netzach, Yesod, and Hod realizations only allow key and combat pages from Star of the City or earlier. Later floors may use owned Impurity pages.&#10;&#10;[First-clear rewards]&#10;A first clear adds that floor's exclusive abnormality pages to later reward pools and makes its E.G.O. eligible after suitable bosses. Replays do not repeat these rewards.&#10;&#10;[Temporary loadout]&#10;Realization teams are temporary; the normal-run setup is restored afterward.</text>
  <text id="ui_RMR_Help_Body_Special">[The Red Mist and Binah]&#10;At Star of the City, the Red Mist may appear as a special elite encounter. Binah becomes temporarily available for the fight. Victory records Binah for later battles and replaces normal rewards with the Red Mist's key and combat pages.&#10;&#10;[The Black Silence]&#10;Clear the Roland boss at Impuritas Civitatis once to record the Black Silence. Future Star of the City runs automatically add its key page to the route.&#10;&#10;[Argalia]&#10;Clear the Distorted Ensemble boss once to record Argalia. Future Star of the City runs automatically add his key and combat pages.</text>
  <text id="ui_RMR_Help_Body_Goal">[Core goal]&#10;RMR is not simply a sequence of base-game receptions. Every run asks you to choose, trade off, and build.&#10;&#10;[Build directions]&#10;Balance new combat and key pages, abnormality pages, passives and other supplies, upgrades, realizations, special characters, E.G.O., and preparation for later bosses.&#10;&#10;[Replay]&#10;As the run advances, available pages, resources, and special characters expand, letting each journey produce a different team and combat style.</text>
